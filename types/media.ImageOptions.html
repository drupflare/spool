    Optionalpng

    png?: PngMode

    How a PNG becomes WebP; default 'lossy'. 'lossless' encodes every PNG without loss, and 'auto' encodes a PNG under 200 KB both ways and serves the smaller (a screenshot or a flat graphic is often smaller lossless, a photograph in a PNG is not). A JPEG is always lossy. The mode is part of the cache key and the ETag of a PNG that is not 'lossy'.

    How long the proxy waits for the encoder, in milliseconds; default 10,000, false to wait without limit. When the time runs out the visitor gets the origin's bytes, and the encoder is left to finish: a result that arrives late is cached for the next request.
