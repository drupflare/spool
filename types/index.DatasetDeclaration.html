    OptionalmaxStaleMs

    maxStaleMs?: number

    Principal datasets only, and required there: how long held rows may answer a query after the last sync that returned and verified. Past it the query goes to the network. It is also the longest a device keeps serving rows after the server stopped admitting its principal.
